Our Services

Comprehensive lawn and landscape care for residential and commercial properties across Long Island.

Year-Round Care

Everything Your Property Needs

We handle every aspect of your lawn and landscape so you can enjoy a beautiful property without the work.

⛺
Lawn Maintenance

Our core service — consistent, reliable lawn care on a schedule that works for you. We mow, edge, trim, and blow every visit to leave your property looking immaculate.

  • Weekly & bi-weekly mowing programs
  • Precision edging along driveways, walkways & beds
  • String trimming around all obstacles
  • Debris blowout after each service
  • Seasonal schedule adjustments
🌿
Landscape Design & Installation

Transform your outdoor space with a custom landscape design. Mark personally consults with every client to understand their vision, budget, and long-term goals.

  • Custom planting plans & design consultations
  • Flower bed creation & annual plantings
  • Shrub & ornamental tree installation
  • Mulching & bed topdressing
  • Landscape lighting concepts
🌳
Tree & Shrub Care

Healthy trees and properly shaped shrubs dramatically improve curb appeal. Our crews are trained in proper pruning techniques that promote growth and plant health.

  • Shrub shaping & pruning
  • Ornamental tree trimming
  • Dead-wood removal
  • Spring & fall shrub maintenance
  • Plant health assessments
❄
Snow Removal

Don't let winter slow you down. We offer reliable snow plowing and ice management services for residential driveways and commercial properties throughout the season.

  • Driveway plowing after each snowfall
  • Walkway clearing & shoveling
  • Ice melt & salt application
  • Seasonal contracts available
  • Per-storm pricing also available
🍂
Spring & Fall Cleanup

Start each season fresh. Our thorough cleanup services remove debris, leaves, and winter damage to prepare your lawn and beds for optimal growth.

  • Full leaf removal & hauling
  • Bed cleaning & edging
  • Dethatching & aeration
  • Debris removal from lawn & beds
  • Property-wide inspection & assessment
💧
Fertilization & Weed Control

A beautiful lawn starts below the surface. Our fertilization programs are customized to your lawn's specific needs, promoting dense, green growth while controlling weeds.

  • Multi-step seasonal fertilization programs
  • Pre- & post-emergent weed control
  • Grub prevention treatments
  • Overseeding for thin or bare areas
  • Soil pH testing & lime applications
🌞
Mulching & Top Dressing

Fresh mulch instantly elevates your property's appearance while protecting plant roots and retaining moisture. We offer a variety of mulch colors and materials.

  • Hardwood & cedar mulch installation
  • Bed edging before mulch installation
  • Top dressing with premium compost
  • Stone & decorative gravel installation
  • Annual mulch refresh programs
🏠
Commercial Property Care

First impressions matter for businesses. We maintain commercial properties, churches, and HOA communities with the same care we bring to every residential property.

  • Commercial mowing & maintenance contracts
  • Parking lot & common area cleanup
  • Church & community property care
  • HOA landscape management
  • Flexible scheduling around business hours
