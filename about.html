Meet the Owner
Mark
Owner & Operator · Independent Lawn Service

Mark founded Independent Lawn Service with a simple belief: every property deserves consistent, high-quality care from someone who genuinely cares about the outcome. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a professional crew serving hundreds of Long Island homes and businesses — but the personal touch has never changed.

Unlike larger impersonal landscaping companies, Mark stays hands-on. He personally responds to calls and texts, consults on landscape projects, and ensures every property in his care meets the standard he'd want for his own home. Customers consistently note that he calls or texts back before they've even hung up the phone.

Mark's philosophy is simple: be honest, do great work, and treat every customer's property like your own. No unnecessary upsells. No shortcuts. Just reliable, professional care you can count on year after year.
